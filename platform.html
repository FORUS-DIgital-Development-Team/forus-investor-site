PLATFORM · THE ASSET BEHIND THE INVESTMENT

One core. Many networks. More value from each deployment.

The core platform investment is already in place, according to management. FORUS can apply that shared foundation to different communities, services and markets, with ongoing development focused on what each deployment needs.

WHY REUSE MATTERS

A lean cost base starts with shared infrastructure.

Identity, transaction coordination, workflows and reporting are common needs across many networks. Reusing those capabilities reduces the need to build a separate technology stack for every opportunity.

Management describes current running costs as very low. The investor question is how that cost base behaves as usage grows: platform hosting, support, provider charges, compliance and local delivery all remain part of the economics.

COMMERCIAL STRUCTURE

Local distribution, shared economics.

FORUS Card Services N.V. owns or licenses the core platform intellectual property and Allocation Engine. FORUS Digital Cooperative is the member governed licensee and custodian of the partner register. Local operators are intended to run approved deployments.

The proposed N.V. allocation applies to defined Platform-Usage Revenue, after payment provider costs and excluding professional services and pass-through costs. Revenue attribution and the final agreements need to be settled.

WHO DOES WHAT

The investment is in one company within the ecosystem.

The proposed ordinary shares are in the N.V.
