01 / PLATFORM

One operating layer, configured for many networks.

FORUS combines identity, wallets, payments, commerce and management tools. A network can start with a specific service, then add activity without rebuilding its core systems.

HOW A DEPLOYMENT WORKS

From a member network to repeat usage.

1

Bring a network

A cooperative, transport body or business partner defines its members, merchants, services and local operating model.

2

Activate shared tools

FORUS configures identity, wallets, transaction flows, management and reporting around the network's use case.

3

Grow activity

More useful services can increase repeat interactions. Platform revenue depends on actual use, agreed pricing and collections.

COMMERCIAL STRUCTURE

Local distribution, shared economics.

FORUS Card Services N.V. owns or licenses the core platform intellectual property and Allocation Engine. FORUS Digital Cooperative is the member governed licensee and custodian of the partner register. Local operators are intended to run approved deployments.

The proposed N.V. allocation applies to defined Platform-Usage Revenue, after payment provider costs and excluding professional services and pass-through costs. Revenue attribution and the final agreements need to be settled.

WHO DOES WHAT

The investment is in one company within the ecosystem.
