03 / INVESTMENT CASE

USD 30m now. Milestone-earned upside on the way to scale.

A worked equity structure makes the trade-off visible: capital funds execution, the investor receives meaningful initial ownership, and founders and the team earn back a defined portion when adoption is proven. No term here is signed or offered.

ILLUSTRATIVE TERMS

How one earn-out could work.

Primary capital; no assumed secondary proceeds.

1

Initial investment

USD 30m into the rights-holding investee for 15% fully diluted equity: USD 170m pre-money and USD 200m post-money in this example. Share class, entity and protections are open.

2

Adoption earn-back

At 5m, 25m and 65m verified paying active users, 2, 2 and 1 percentage points respectively transfer from the investor stake to a founder/team pool. The investor retains 13%, 11% or 10% as gates are met.

3

Exit participation

At a hypothetical USD 20bn equity exit, an undiluted 10% investor stake yields USD 2bn, or 66.7× the initial USD 30m. Remaining holders share USD 18bn according to their actual cap table.

This is a suggested negotiation framework, not the August 2026 transaction. Milestones, vesting window, share-transfer mechanics, change-of-control treatment, governance and tax must be agreed.

A proposed “paid active user” is a unique person who completed at least one qualifying paid platform transaction with collected attributable revenue in the trailing 12 months. No duplicate users across programmes. An independent verifier would check data and revenue, with an agreed time window, anti-gaming rules and treatment on an early sale. These thresholds are illustrative and are not a current forecast.

THE USD 20BN GAP

The current unit assumption alone does not support that exit.

The August model assumed USD 1.75 annual N.V. revenue retained per active user. If every one of the pipeline's 65m full-potential users became paying active users, annual retained revenue would be USD 113.75m. At an illustrative 20× revenue multiple, that is USD 2.275bn of equity value, before accounting for costs, debt or valuation differences.

A USD 20bn equity value at 20× would require USD 1bn annual retained revenue: about 571m paying active users at USD 1.75 each, or USD 15.38 retained per user across 65m. That requires additional monetisation, substantially more adoption, a different multiple or a combination. None has been demonstrated.

DEPLOYMENT OF CAPITAL

Fund a measurable operating plan.

The USD 30m is a new proposal, with no approved allocation in the August pack. A detailed budget should be prepared for product hardening, signed partner deployments, local integrations, compliance, sales and operating runway. Release against documented delivery and reporting milestones rather than presenting invented spending amounts.

The previous USD 7.5m secondary sale and shareholder loan were described in the August 2026 materials. They are not included in this illustrative primary raise. Existing debt, licences and any related-party transactions still require reconciliation.
