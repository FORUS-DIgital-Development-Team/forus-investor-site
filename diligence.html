04 / INVESTOR READINESS

Issues to resolve before an investment.

A practical action list from the August 2026 investor materials. Each item states what investors need to see. The status of these items should be refreshed with current documents and operating data. FORUS is pre-revenue; the USD 30m structure and USD 20bn ambition are illustrative.

PRIORITY ONE · TRANSACTION FOUNDATION

Settle the rights and the capital structure.

These issues determine what an investor would actually buy.

01

Execute the commercial agreements

The Master Joint Venture Agreement was a draft. Finalise the platform licence, operator arrangements, revenue definition, partner register, attribution method and schedules; obtain required member approval.

Close with: signed agreements, approvals and an effective date.
02

Define the shareholder loan

The August secondary-sale plan contemplated a shareholder loan. Reconcile whether any loan survives the new USD 30m primary-raise concept and disclose all debt, security, related-party claims and cash priorities.

Close with: an updated debt schedule, revised capital plan and signed terms for any surviving loan.
03

Prove the equity valuation and cap table

The new worked example uses USD 30m for 15%, implying USD 200m post-money. Reconcile the full cap table, rights-holding entity, historical results, assets, liabilities, existing debt, option pool, later financing and realistic adoption assumptions.

Close with: current cap table, financial statements, debt schedule and an evidence-backed valuation case.
04

Agree minority investor protections and exit

The new earn-back requires a precise share transfer or escrow mechanism, verified milestones, timing, governance, investor protections, change-of-control treatment and an exit route. Board rights, preferences and later dilution remain open.

Close with: negotiated shareholders' agreement and amended constitutional documents.

PRIORITY TWO · OPERATING PROOF

Show that the model earns and collects revenue.

Pipeline reach becomes valuable when it converts into sustained activity.

05

Publish WOW pilot operating results

Supply the number of operators and users, transactions, fuel volume and rebates, realised revenue, costs, repeat use and settlement outcomes.

Close with: a reconciled pilot report and a rollout unit-economics model.
06

Convert pipeline estimates into committed launches

The August pipeline's 5.4m minimum and 65m potential were partner-stated reach estimates. They need scope, dates, pricing, responsibilities and measurable activation milestones.

Close with: signed partner schedules and a stage-by-stage conversion forecast.
07

Validate annual revenue per active user

The model assumes USD 10 gross annual platform usage revenue per user and USD 1.75 retained by the N.V. after the stated allocation. Actual transactions, provider costs and collections must test this. Confirm whether USD 10 represents gross revenue or the amount eligible for allocation after provider costs.

Close with: transaction-led ARPU, margin, churn and cohort evidence.
08

Clarify deployment costs and capacity

Partner onboarding, local rail integration, support, compliance and country operations affect the cost of scaling a shared platform.

Close with: implementation budgets, service levels, operating responsibilities and a funded rollout plan.

PRIORITY THREE · CONTROL AND APPROVALS

Remove legal and execution uncertainty.

09

Verify IP ownership, licence and escrow

Trace ownership or valid licensing of the core platform and Allocation Engine. Resolve the Curaçao insolvency licence and source-code escrow questions raised in the pack.

Close with: chain-of-title records, signed licence and independent legal advice.
10

Obtain required regulatory and exchange-control positions

Confirm permissions for local payments and operations, the treatment of MahalaX, and South African exchange-control approval affecting offshore settlement.

Close with: jurisdiction-specific legal opinions, approvals and a launch compliance matrix.
11

Document the Cooperative advance

If any advance to the Cooperative remains in the new plan, its authority, repayment source, security, relationship to the investee and cross-border treatment need definition.

Close with: a signed loan agreement and repayment analysis if included, or a formal removal from the funding plan.
12

Reconcile and update the investor model

Rebuild the August model for a USD 30m primary raise, earn-back and funding runway. Correct its old 1.0× annotation: USD 100m / USD 1.75 is 57.1m users, not “approximately 50 million”. Validate revenue, costs, churn, future dilution and the USD 20bn bridge.

Close with: a version-controlled model tied to current operating evidence.

These are unresolved items identified or implied by the August 2026 memorandum, financial model, annexure and closing checklist. This site has not verified which items may have since been completed.
