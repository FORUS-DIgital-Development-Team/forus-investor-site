WHY THE OPPORTUNITY IS COMPELLING

More activity on the same foundation.

FORUS combines identity, payments, commerce and network operations on a shared technology base. A deployment can build on that foundation, bringing new participants and services into an existing platform.

That is the economic attraction: grow useful activity while keeping the operating structure lean. If retained revenue grows faster than the costs of serving it, a greater share can become operating cash.
