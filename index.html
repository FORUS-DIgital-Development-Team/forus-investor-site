FORUS DIGITAL · INVESTOR OVERVIEW

Build once. Activate networks. Share the upside.

FORUS is a pre-revenue platform venture. We have built reusable infrastructure for identity, wallets, payments and operations, and assembled a large partner-stated pipeline. The investment thesis is to turn those relationships into contracted deployments, paying active users and repeat transactions.

THE INVESTMENT THESIS

Distribution could turn a shared core into a network business.

Cooperatives, transport networks and other communities already organise people and commerce. FORUS supplies common technology while partners contribute local relationships and use cases. A single platform can serve multiple deployments, but each must prove adoption, revenue collection and economics.

Current pipeline is an opportunity set, not revenue. The first funded phase should turn selected programmes into signed, measured rollouts and validate the contractual share of platform usage revenue.
